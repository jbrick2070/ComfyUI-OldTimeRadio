# Codex brief: harden the widget-drift plan

Paste the fenced block into Codex. Everything above it is the grounding.

Written 2026-09-14. The widget tier is planned but **not coded**. This asks for
the plan to be attacked before any of it is executed.

---

## Why this brief exists

The plan already survived one independent QA (Cursor) and four Sonnet rounds on
the *surrounding* work. What it has NOT had is a reader whose only job is to
break the **drift model** -- the theory of how a widget change propagates and
what catches it.

That theory has been wrong twice already this week:

* `test_workflow_link_target_indexes` was believed to guard all 17 graphs. It
  used a non-recursive glob and collected **one item**. Two years of confidence
  in a check that had never looked at a variant.
* "A trailing widget is nearly free" was believed on inspection. Measured, it is
  the *worst* case: `migrateWidgetsValues` fires at exactly `k == 1`, so
  removing one trailing widget corrupts `replay_from` and the three My Story
  fields, while removing the last **two** corrupts nothing.

Both were confident, both were wrong, both were invisible to a green suite.

## The facts, measured

`workflows/otr_canonical.json` is the source of truth; the 16 files in
`workflows/variants/` are projections regenerated by
`scripts/build_variants.py --all` and are never hand-edited.

* `OTR_LedgerScriptWriter`: 38 declared inputs, 37 widgets, one `forceInput`
  (`gate_in`, declaration index 32, carrying link 279).
* 2,074 widget descriptors across the 17 shipped graphs.
* `config/profiles/widget_mapping.json`: 47 managed keys, **55** managed targets.
* ComfyUI frontend 1.51.10, backend 0.35.1.
* `Comfy.Workflow.NamedValuesRestore` is `defaultValue: false, experimental: true`,
  so restore is a **positional zip** of `widgets_values` onto the live widget
  list. Names do not participate.
* `fallbackWidgetsValuesNames` exists in the frontend's node-def schema but the
  backend never emits it -- `server.py::node_info` has no such key.
* Three widget descriptors carry a live `link` today: `OTR_SceneSequencer`'s
  `script_json` (277), and `OTR_SignalLostVideo`'s `script_json` (16) and
  `news_used` (110).

## What already exists

* `scripts/otr_widget_surgery.py` -- the three-part procedure as a module.
  `remove_widget` and `reorder_widgets` both return `(touched, repairs)` and
  repair the link table themselves; both refuse a `widgets_values` that is short
  or absent. `repair_dst_slots` is identity-based and idempotent.
* `tests/test_widget_surgery_tool.py` -- 14 tests against the real canonical.
* `docs/2026-09-13-widget-cleanup-QA-VERDICT.md` -- the plan of record.
* `docs/HANDOFF_WIDGET_TIER_EXECUTE.md` -- the execution handoff.
