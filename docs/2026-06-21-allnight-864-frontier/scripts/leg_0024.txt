# Names On The Board

Tonight, a half-billion-dollar eye on the heavens slips toward the dark, and three rivals race the clock-but only the boldest dares to reach up and catch a falling star.

Then push it - because a company that dies wondering is just a story somebody else tells, and I'd rather she go up screaming than have us standing here polishing the obituary; arm her, the rest of us don't exist until she flies.
