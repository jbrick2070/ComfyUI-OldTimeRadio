*** START OF THE PROJECT GUTENBERG EBOOK THE TIME MACHINE ***

The room had been ordinary a moment before: lamplight, nervous chairs, and
men trying to laugh away a machine they could not explain. Then the traveler
staggered in, pale with dust and weather, and the brass-and-crystal device
stood behind him like proof from an impossible century.

He did not ask them to believe at once. He asked for food, water, and a few
minutes of silence. When his hands stopped shaking, he described the lever,
the rushing years, the lonely garden of the far future, and the terrible
underworld that had taught him what comfort can cost.

The witnesses looked from his torn coat to the machine. Doubt remained, but
it had changed shape. It was no longer easy mockery. It was fear.

*** END OF THE PROJECT GUTENBERG EBOOK THE TIME MACHINE ***
