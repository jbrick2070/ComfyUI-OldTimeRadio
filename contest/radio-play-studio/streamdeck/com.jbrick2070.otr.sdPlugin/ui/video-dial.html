This control

Steps through every video engine the chosen machine films with: LTX 2.5 with foley, plain LTX video, stills, AnimateDiff, LTX 2.5 mime and audio-in, and the visualiser that needs no video model, as far as that machine has them. A turn of the dial, or a press of the key, only rests on an engine (outlined white); press the dial, or hold the key, to switch the Studio to it. An engine marked not yet proven has no published episode on that machine yet. The Machine control chooses the machine.
