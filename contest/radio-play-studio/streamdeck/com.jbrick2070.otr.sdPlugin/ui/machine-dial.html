Waiting for the Studio…

This control

Steps through the machines the Studio knows (8 GB, 16 GB, Mac, AMD, the clouds). A turn of the dial, or a press of the key, only rests on a machine (outlined white); press the dial, or hold the key, to switch the Studio to it. Under the machine is the video engine it would film with: the one you are on when that machine has it, else its first. The Video control chooses the engine. With no menu (the Studio was started on a graph that is not on it), there is nothing to switch.
