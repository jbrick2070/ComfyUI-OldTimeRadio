Radio Play Studio

Press the button. Your own ComfyUI writes, casts, performs and films a radio play.

The play is written, cast and voiced as usual, then it stops after the stills: one picture for every line. Change any picture, try one again, keep the ones you like, then film. Filming is the slow part, so it only films what changed.
