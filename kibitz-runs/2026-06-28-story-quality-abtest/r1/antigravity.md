However, `register_overlap_ratio` (defined in `scripts/story_quality_scan.py#L424-L437` and `nodes/_otr_casting.py#L1429-L1438`) calculates the token overlap strictly over the static `speech_signature` metadata strings assigned to cast members during the casting phase (e.g. "measured, precise").    Concrete Fix: Correct the target metric. Define a text-based vocabulary overlap metric over the actual generated lines in the ledger, or update `register_overlap_ratio` in `scripts/story_quality_scan.py` to evaluate the generated dialogue text.

3. [S4] The plan proposes a "deterministic de-cliche rewrite that drops the phrase" if creative rerolls fail. Dropping matched cliché phrases inline (e.g., stripping "Not on my watch" from "Not on my watch, Lemmy." or "over my dead body" from "We will do this over my dead body.") is grammatically destructive and will produce ungrammatical stubs (", Lemmy." or "We will do this.").
   Concrete Fix: Abandon inline cliché dropping. If a cliché cannot be cleared after two targeted LLM rerolls, either accept the first draft or replace the entire line with a pre-validated generic fallback line mapping to the beat's intent.

4. [S3] The plan proposes extending the "roster-vocative leak rule to detect+repair an embedded ALL-CAPS roster full-name ANYWHERE in the line." An embedded full-name in the middle of a clause is typically a grammatical subject or object (e.g. "...when CLARISSE GORDON claim..."), not a vocative address. Performing a deterministic string drop mid-clause destroys the syntax of the sentence ("...when claim...").
   Concrete Fix: Do not perform in-place regex stripping of embedded full-names mid-clause. If an embedded ALL-CAPS roster name is detected in a non-vocative position, set `needs_recompose = True` in `verify_and_repair_line` to trigger a creative reroll instead of attempting a destructive in-place strip.
