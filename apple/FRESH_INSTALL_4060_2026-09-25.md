not only the failures. Machine: RTX 4060 Laptop, 8 GB VRAM. HEAD at start:
c292539cd475d29bf9315849195ae008152f6180. All actions performed through the
Comfy Desktop app UI (mouse/keyboard), not a headless launcher, unless a step
says otherwise.

outside knowledge. BUG = something that is actually broken.
