## Video
### DMAD (ZhengmingYu/DMAD) -- WATCH (new to SEEN)
- https://huggingface.co/ZhengmingYu/DMAD (card and file list read; updated 2026-10-30; 8-step student added 2026-10-29). Paper arXiv 2610.02188, code https://github.com/Yzmblog/DMAD (not read).
- What: rank-128 distillation LoRAs on MiniMax-H3 that turn the 50-step teacher into 4-step or 8-step sampling, CFG 1.0, 1344x768, 124 frames at 24 fps, native stereo audio. ComfyUI-layout files load with stock LoraLoaderModelOnly, lcm sampler, simple scheduler, ModelSamplingMiniMaxH3 shift 12 / audio shift 2 (or the author's DMAD Sampler nodes). Compare with Pika H3 Flex (6 steps).
- Files (ungated): minimax_h3/dmad_minimax_h3_4step_full_critic_comfyui.safetensors, ..._4step_lora_critic_comfyui, ..._8step_full_critic_comfyui, each 1.96 GB (bf16-sized). Diffusers-layout copies 1.38 GB. Licence: MiniMax H3 community licence (card: license other).
- Sweet-spot build: LoRA itself none (no int8 LoRA). Base side YES: Comfy-Org/MiniMax-H3 pruned int8_convrot (see 10-29 report). Community INT8 ConvRot H3 repacks also exist (PulpCut/MiniMax-H3-INT8-ConvRot, not inspected).
- 16 GB fit: author claims (not measured by me): 16 GB GPUs run the ComfyUI workflows in 14 GiB, 5 s on 8 GB with --low-vram, 15 s in 12 GiB (card, 10-08 and 10-10 updates).
- ComfyUI: no new code needed; H3 support is in tagged core (newest tag v0.43.2). Optional DMAD nodes are a custom pack inside the code repo.
- Risk: the card describes text-to-audio-video students; the repo downloads exclude the FL2VA and Ref2VA transformers, so whether these LoRAs apply to the fl2va / ref2va lanes OTR ships is unverified. Face and lip-sync stability at 4 to 8 steps is unknown.
- Verdict: WATCH. Becomes TEST if the author or a tester shows it on fl2va / ref2va; until then Pika H3 Flex (which targets those two) is the better first probe.
