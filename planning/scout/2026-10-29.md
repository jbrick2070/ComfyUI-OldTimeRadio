## Upscalers
### MiniMax-H3 latent upscaler -- WATCH (SEEN row 10-27 was the Gavining copy; material change: core support)
- Core commit 35060f01 "Support MiniMax-H3 latent upscaler model" (kijai, 10-28), node in comfy_extras/nodes_hunyuan.py. Tag: none (master-only).
- Weights: https://huggingface.co/LBH-123-AI/Minimax_h3_latent_Upscaler (card read; Apache 2.0, ungated; 457 likes, ~297k downloads per the Hub listing): 3D-conv latent upscaler, bf16 and fp16 safetensors about 691 MB each, fp32 .pth 1.38 GB. Works on H3's 24-channel latents, spatial only. Sweet-spot int8: none (and unneeded at 691 MB).
- Why: lets an H3 lane generate low-res and upscale in latent space, skipping a decode/encode round trip; the card claims a time saving and fewer ghosting artefacts than bilinear latent resize. Authors' claim, not measured by me. Needs a refine resample afterwards. OTR config/workflow_matrix.json currently uses rtx_vsr as the pixel upscale_stage engine, so this would be an A/B against that on H3 lanes. 16 GB fit: model is small; estimate fine. Verdict: WATCH, promote to TEST when a core tag carries it.
