## Image
### P-H3-Image (PrunaAI) -- TEST
- https://huggingface.co/PrunaAI/P-H3-Image-Comfy (card read; appeared 2026-11-03; launch post https://www.pruna.ai/blog/oss-p-h3-image-launch, not read, card only).
- What: MiniMax-H3 video DiT turned into an image model: text-to-image at 8 MP in 8 steps, no CFG, and editing with up to 8 input images (image_1 is edited, the rest are references, prompt refers to <Picture N>). Would challenge flux3_klein_ref (max 3 refs) and moss_image on the 16 GB graphs. Includes a Qwen3.5-9B prompt upsampler that the card says is needed (the model is prompt-sensitive).
- Sweet-spot build: YES, ungated, in the model's own repo. diffusion_models/p-h3-image_int8_convrot.safetensors 21.2 GB (https://huggingface.co/PrunaAI/P-H3-Image-Comfy/resolve/main/diffusion_models/p-h3-image_int8_convrot.safetensors); text encoder int8_convrot 5.1 GB; VAE int8_convrot 3.1 GB; upsampler int8_convrot 9.7 GB. Also w4a8: model 13.4 GB, encoder 3.6 GB (4-bit codebook, changes more detail). bf16 40.5 GB. LoRA (2.0 GB) runs it on an existing MiniMax-H3 FL2VA model.
- 16 GB fit: author figures (Pruna, from the card): smallest set (w4a8 model and encoder, int8 VAE) 20 GB download, peak 21 GB VRAM at 8 MP; int8 set 30 GB download, peak 31 GB. So it does NOT fit 16 GB without ComfyUI offload; whether offload at 4 MP edits stays practical is unmeasured (my estimate: w4a8 at 4 MP edits is the only plausible 16 GB case). 8 GB: no.
- ComfyUI requirement: core only, no custom nodes. Commit 576b642e "Support PH3Image" (kijai, 2026-11-03) is in tag v0.44.0 (git tag --contains confirms). The box runs tagged releases, so v0.44.0 is enough.
- Licence: MiniMax H3 Community License (not a gate here; note it excludes EU, UK, Korea and US without a separate licence, and requires "Powered by MiniMax H3"); Qwen parts Apache-2.0. Text is unreliable for small text, hands can break (card limitations).
- Why: it is the first image engine with 8-reference editing in core, relevant to character consistency across beats. Risk: size and the licence terms; the card's quality claims are the authors' own, nothing measured by us.
- Test to propose: w4a8 set, 4 MP edit with two or three character portraits as references, compare face hold against flux3_klein_ref on the 5080, by eye.

## Upscalers
### RelayVSR X2 (lightx2v) -- WATCH
- https://huggingface.co/lightx2v/RelayVSR_X2 (card read, updated 2026-11-02; first seen on the news page 10-29).
- What: fixed 2x video super-resolution trained to suppress temporal flicker in generated video. Author benchmark (measured by lightx2v, one H100 80GB, 243 frames at 1024x768 to 2048x1536): 5.35 s for X2 versus 352.8 s for SeedVR2-3B and 54 s for FlashVSR+ Turbo. Could in principle give the 8 GB LTX lanes (832x480) a bigger picture, but not measured on small cards.
- Files: wan_x2.safetensors 10.1 GB plus flash_x2.safetensors 128 MB. Sweet-spot build: none yet. Runs in LightX2V's own scripts; I found no ComfyUI core loader or node pack for it (not through UpscaleModelLoader/spandrel). Licence not stated on the card. Verdict WATCH until a ComfyUI node exists.
