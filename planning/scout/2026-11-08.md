### TimeCapsuleLLM-ja v3 (trtd56) -- WATCH
- https://huggingface.co/trtd56/TimeCapsuleLLM-ja-v3 (card read, Japanese). Trained from scratch, decoder-only GPT, 730,164,480 parameters, safetensors with its own inference.py (torch, tokenizers, safetensors). Corpus: Japanese texts 1868-1945 (Aozora Bunko works passing era and rights filters, plus pre-1945 public-domain NDL OCR material), 2.74 B unique tokens, 8.23 B processed. Apache-2.0. Author-reported: pre-war test BPB 0.9922 versus 1.6698 on modern text. No sample sentence on the card; the card says it is not a chat model and may repeat or emit OCR typos.
- 16 GB fit: trivial (about 1.5 GB bf16, estimate). 
- Experiment: period Japanese continuation for the ja lane (Japanese is one of OTR's eight languages); a base model, so it would need a continuation-style prompt, not instructions.
