### ReCaVSR (USTC; ComfyUI int8 repack by PedroMarinhoDev) -- TEST
- Link: https://huggingface.co/PedroMarinhoDev/ReCaVSR-ComfyUI (2026-11-10; card read). Nodes: https://github.com/PedroMarinhoDev/ComfyUI-ReCaVSR (registry id comfyui-recavsr 2.0.0, 2026-11-10). Original weights https://huggingface.co/kopper/ReCaVSR.
- What: one-step streaming diffusion video super-resolution on Wan2.2 TI2V-5B, 4x, 8 frames per block.
- Sweet-spot build: transformer_int8_convrot 6.27 GB (bf16 10.6 GB). Needs prompt.safetensors and flashdecoder.safetensors from kopper/ReCaVSR. Licence "other" (recavsr-weights).
- Speed and fit (measured by the repack author, RTX 4090, ComfyUI 0.44, torch 2.14): 149 frames 960x480 to 3840x1920 in 36 s int8 vs 49 s bf16; int8 is 48.0 dB against bf16; about 4 GB less VRAM than bf16. 3090: 67 s. No 5080 or 8 GB figure.
- Risk: custom node pack (not core), 4x is more than the lanes need and may over-sharpen; torch 2.14 in their test vs OTR's 2.10. A probe on the same clip as RelayVSR gives a direct A/B.
