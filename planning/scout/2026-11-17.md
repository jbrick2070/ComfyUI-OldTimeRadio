## Video
### LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.6 (meituan-longcat, 2026-11-13) -- WATCH
- Link: https://huggingface.co/meituan-longcat/LongCat-Video-Avatar-1.6 (card read, partial: intro, human eval, usage tips; file list read).
- What it does: audio + text (+ image) to talking-avatar video, single and multi character, long continuation past 10 minutes with stable identity, calmer lip animation than 1.5. Languages on card: en, zh. Would challenge/add to the H3 ref2va and ltx25_audio_in lanes for lip-synced talking heads. Card's own human-preference study (33,051 votes, author figure): second of six, behind OmniHuman-2.0, ahead of HeyGen and Kling Avatar 2.0.
- Sweet-spot build: official repo has a weight-only int8 DiT (base_model_int8, about 15.9 GB over 4 shards, enabled with --use_int8 in their own code). Community TeamAIQ INT8-ConvRot (ungated, MIT, appeared 2026-11-17): https://huggingface.co/TeamAIQ/LongCat-Video-Avatar-1.6-INT8-ConvRot, `diffusion_pytorch_model_int8_convrot.safetensors` 16.9 GB, plus DMD and OPSD LoRAs 2.5 GB each. The card says it loads in Wan2GP on Comfy Kitchen INT8 kernels; it does not mention ComfyUI core.
- 16 GB fit: no. 16.9 GB of transformer weights alone exceeds the 14.5 GB ceiling; would need block offload. bf16 base is 31.7 GB. No published VRAM number read; estimate only. 8 GB: no.
- ComfyUI: no core support found. A community pack ComfyUI-LongCat-Avatar covers 1.5 per a web summary (not read); GGUF repacks exist for 1.5 (vantagewithai). No 1.6 pack seen.
- Official quick start installs flash-attn 2, which does not exist for this stack (FA2 can be swapped for xformers/SDPA per the card, untested here).
- Why it might matter: long, identity-stable, audio-driven talking video is the weak spot of character-per-beat video. Main risk: size, English/Chinese only, no core loader.
- Verdict: WATCH until a Comfy-Org repack or a loader on core appears, or a GGUF/smaller quant of 1.6.
