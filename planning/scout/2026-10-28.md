## Voice / TTS

### Audio8-TTS-Realistic-0.6B (Edge0) -- WATCH
- Link: https://huggingface.co/Edge0/Audio8-TTS-Realistic-0.6b (updated 2026-10-27; card read). Paper arXiv 2610.14776, code https://github.com/Edge0-AI/Audio8_TTS
- What: 601M-parameter DualAR TTS (design inspired by Fish S2 Pro), 44.1 kHz bundled codec, zero-shot voice cloning from a reference clip plus its exact transcript, also plain generation.
- Languages (card): 23, including en, es, fr, it, pt, ja, zh and hi, so all eight of OTR's languages. Card warns quality varies by language.
- Published numbers (authors, measured): Seed-TTS error 1.25 %, speaker similarity 0.704; MiniMax multilingual 24-language error 5.89 %, similarity 0.754. These are intelligibility and similarity metrics, not a naturalness score. No real-time factor is published; throughput was explicitly not benchmarked.
- Sweet-spot build: none. Files are model.safetensors (1.2 GB, so bf16) plus codec.pth (1.35 GB). No int8 repack found.
- 16 GB fit: estimate well under 8 GB (about 2.6 GB of weights plus KV cache). Both boxes plausible.
- ComfyUI requirement: no core support, no node pack found. Uses transformers with trust_remote_code (transformers 4.57.x, below 5), so it needs a wrapper node.
- Licence: Apache 2.0, ungated.
- Risk: new, custom code, no speed number, no ear evidence for period delivery. Verdict: WATCH (a wrapper node or any speed number would make it TEST; it would sit beside KittenTTS 2 from 10-27).

## Video

### Bernini-R (ByteDance, Comfy-Org repack) -- WATCH (not a new model; new to SEEN)
- Links: https://huggingface.co/ByteDance/Bernini-R (card read; released 2026-06-01), repack https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/Bernini-R (card and file list read).
- What: Wan 2.2 A14B-based video editing / generation renderer driven by a semantic planner. Comfy-Org ships Video Edit and Image Edit templates (video_bernini_r_video_editing, video_bernini_r_image_editing).
- Sweet-spot build: YES. wan2.2_bernini_r_high_noise_int8_convrot.safetensors and wan2.2_bernini_r_low_noise_int8_convrot.safetensors, 14.5 GB each (https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/Bernini-R/tree/main/diffusion_models). Also fp8_scaled 15.6 GB and mxfp8 15.0 GB. Ungated, Apache 2.0.
- 16 GB fit: each expert is 14.5 GB, so it runs one expert at a time with swapping; that sits at the 14.5 GB ceiling before text encoder and activations. Estimate, not measured by anyone I found. 8 GB: no.
- ComfyUI requirement: Comfy-Org templates exist; I found no Bernini-specific core commit in the last 45 days, so support is probably older. Not confirmed which tag.
- Why it might matter: video editing / restyle of a finished beat. OTR has no video-edit lane. Risk: heavy, off-lane, and the authors' arena results are self-run.
- Verdict: WATCH.

## Image

### Boogu-Image-0.1 (Boogu; Comfy-Org repack) -- WATCH
- Links: https://huggingface.co/Boogu/Boogu-Image-0.1-Edit (card read, Apache 2.0, research release, tech report arXiv 2607.13125, family: Base, Turbo, Edit, Edit-Turbo); repack https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/Boogu-Image (card and file list read). Model dates from July 2026; the repack was touched 10-28. First time in SEEN.
- What: unified text-to-image and instruction image editing, English and Chinese text rendering. Edit variants are the ones that could challenge flux3_klein_ref for character-consistent stills.
- Sweet-spot build: YES. boogu_image_edit_int8_convrot.safetensors 11.4 GB, boogu_image_edit_turbo_int8_convrot.safetensors 11.4 GB, plus a hotfix turbo int8_convrot at 10.3 GB (https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/Boogu-Image/tree/main/diffusion_models). Also fp8_scaled 10.3 GB and nvfp4 5.8 GB (nvfp4 suits the Blackwell 5080). Text encoder qwen3vl_8b_fp8_scaled is 10.6 GB, VAE 168 MB. Ungated.
- 16 GB fit: transformer 11.4 GB plus an 10.6 GB encoder means the encoder must be offloaded or run sequentially; estimate, not measured. nvfp4 transformer (5.8 GB) is the 8 GB route, quality unknown.
- ComfyUI requirement: Comfy-Org templates (image_boogu_image_0_1_edit_int8) imply native support; no core commit in the last 45 days, tag unconfirmed.
- Risk: no evidence on face retention across edits; the maker's own cards say an earlier edit-turbo had "severe image quality degradation" fixed in a July hotfix. Verdict: WATCH (a face-hold A/B against Klein-ref on one character sheet is the cheap test; promote to TEST if the operator wants it).
