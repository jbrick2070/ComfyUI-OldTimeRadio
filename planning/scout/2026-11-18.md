## Video
### FastFrames-S / FastFrames-L (Apple, appeared 2026-11-18) -- SKIP
- Link: https://huggingface.co/apple/FastFrames-L and https://huggingface.co/apple/FastFrames-S (cards read, files listed).
- What it does: autoregressive two-step text-to-video, 832x480, 81 frames at 16 fps. S is 1.3B (Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B base), L is 14B (Wan2.1-T2V-14B base). Apple's own latency table: 35.4 ms for S on 1 GPU (author figure, GPU not stated in the part read).
- Sweet-spot build: none. L: dit.safetensors 28.6 GB (about 14B in bf16 by size) plus a 1.78 GB KV refiner and 0.29 GB pixel decoder; S DiT weights were not in the listing I read (only kv_refiner and pixel_decoder, 0.69 GB and 0.26 GB).
- 16 GB fit: L no (28.6 GB, README benchmarks it on 8 GPUs). S plausible by size, estimate only. 
- ComfyUI: no core support, no pack seen. Needs their own uv/CUDA 12.8 repo.
- Licence: apple-amlr. Text-to-video only: no image, audio or reference input, so no fit for ref2va or lip-sync lanes.
- Verdict: SKIP.

## Writer LLMs (experimental)
### wieszcz-xix-349m (SKocur, 2026-10-19) -- WATCH
- Link: https://huggingface.co/SKocur/wieszcz-xix-349m (card read to the content-warning section; file list read). Corpus: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SKocur/polish-pre1918-corpus
- Base model: none, trained from scratch (Llama/Gemma-2 style: 24 layers, dim 1152, GQA, byte-level BPE vocab 8000). 349,069,440 parameters, base model (continues text, does not follow instructions), context 1024 tokens.
- Corpus and cutoff: Polish text catalogued 1800-1918, 6.69B training tokens; paper reports a ladder 47M / 107M / 349M (perplexity 14.48 on its own held-out split, author figure).
- Formats: safetensors (model.safetensors 1.40 GB, appears fp32). No GGUF seen. Needs `trust_remote_code` (custom model.py) or the card's direct loader (torch, safetensors, tokenizers).
- 16 GB fit: yes by a wide margin (1.4 GB; estimate).
- Licence: apache-2.0. Card carries a content warning (period prejudice).
- Sample: the card gives only a prompt, "Doniesiono nam z Warszawy, iz" (a news-style opener), no generated sample.
- Experiment: a period-Polish continuation voice for a Polish-language episode. Risk: Polish is outside OTR's eight languages, base-only, 1024 context, and small models like this ramble; it is a language curiosity rather than a lane.
