**TypewriterLM 1913 (typewriter-ai; paper arXiv 2606.02991) -- TEST** (in SEEN only as "news only" WATCH on 2026-10-27; now real weights found, so reported again)
- Links: https://huggingface.co/typewriter-ai/typewriter-1913-7B-sft-v2 (also -base-v2, -dpo-v2, typewriter-1913-v2-selfinstruct); paper https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02991 ; community GGUF https://huggingface.co/foss22/TypeWriter-1913-7B-sft-v2-TQ-GGUF . Repos dated 2026-07-14 to 08-28.
- Base/size: Llama architecture (config read), 7.24B params, 32k vocab, 8k context; trained on 54B tokens of "TypewriterCorpus" with heavy temporal filtering, knowledge cutoff 1913 (paper abstract, read this run). Likely from scratch (paper says pretraining; not confirmed whether from scratch).
- Formats: safetensors bf16 model.safetensors 14.48 GB (measured from listing); GGUF TQ3_4S 3.67 GB (community, nonstandard quant name, may need a llama.cpp fork: unverified).
- 16 GB fit: bf16 at 14.5 GB sits right at the box ceiling with no KV room; estimate only, so use the GGUF or an 8-bit load for a real run. The GGUF fits 8 GB too (estimate).
- Licence: not stated on the repos (no model card in the sft or base repos); paper is CC BY 4.0. Corpus and cutoff year: pre-1913 text. Sample sentence: none shown (no card).
- Experiment: drive a pre-WWI voice and worldview baked into weights, for example a 1910-era "radio play" scene with no knowledge of later events, and compare with the shipped Gemma 4 12B prompted for period voice. Runs via transformers safetensors or llama.cpp. The operator picks and downloads by hand.
- Risk: no card, unknown licence, SFT data may be synthetic; the dpo/sft/selfinstruct variants are not described. Verdict TEST (experiment only, not a prose-quality claim).
