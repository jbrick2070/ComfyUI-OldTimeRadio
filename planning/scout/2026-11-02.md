## Image
### JoyAI-Image-Edit-Plus (JD.com) -- WATCH
- https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/JoyAI-Image-Edit (repack card read); original https://huggingface.co/jdopensource/JoyAI-Image-Edit-Plus-Diffusers (card read). Core support commit acf5a145 (2026-10-23) is in tags v0.43.0, v0.43.1, v0.43.2. Template image_joyai_image_edit exists.
- What: multi-reference instruction image editing (combine people/scene from several images). 16B MMDiT + Qwen3-VL-8B encoder + Wan 2.1 VAE. Would challenge flux3_klein_ref for character-consistent stills; no identity-fidelity numbers in the card.
- Sweet-spot build: yes. diffusion_models/joyai_image_edit_int8_convrot.safetensors 16.43 GB; text_encoders/qwen3vl_8b_joyimage_edit_int8_convrot.safetensors 10.06 GB (bf16: 32.5 GB + 17.5 GB). Ungated, Apache-2.0.
- 16 GB fit: estimate only, no published VRAM: the int8 DiT alone is about 16.4 GB, so it needs weight offload to system RAM and will be slow; 8 GB not realistic. Card recommends 30 steps, guidance 4.
- Risk: size and speed; identity quality unmeasured. A probe against Klein-ref on one character sheet would settle it.

## Writer LLMs (experimental)
### MonadGPT (Pclanglais) -- WATCH
- https://huggingface.co/Pclanglais/MonadGPT (card read). Base teknium/OpenHermes-2-Mistral-7B (Mistral 7B), full fine-tune on about 11,000 early modern texts (EEBO, Gallica) in English, French, Latin, roughly 1400-1700. Formats: safetensors, plus GGUF (TheBloke/MonadGPT-GGUF, QuantFactory/MonadGPT-GGUF), AWQ, GPTQ. 7B fits 16 GB in bf16 (about 14.5 GB, estimate) or easily as Q4/Q8 GGUF. Apache-2.0, ungated. First noted 10-27 as "unread"; now read.
- Sample from the card: "The six Planets are, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, and the Sun."
- Experiment: period voice and dated worldview (pre-Newton science) for a Shakespeare-era or alchemist adaptation lane. It is a fine-tune of a modern base, not clean-room, so leakage of modern knowledge is possible. Run via transformers or llama.cpp by hand.
