## Voice / TTS
### ZONOS3-Mini (Zyphra) -- TEST
- Link: https://huggingface.co/Zyphra/ZONOS3-Mini (appeared 2026-11-10; card read, README plus file list).
- What: compact TTS with voice cloning from a reference clip (up to 60 s) or a 192-d speaker embedding, character design by age/accent/texture, per-word steering, 44.1 kHz output. 800M params total, about 100M active (latent MoE), 15 Hz codec frames. Would challenge or sit beside Kokoro (which has no cloning).
- Languages: text normalization for en, zh, ja, ko, de, es, fr, it, pt (others pass through as written). Default voices (27) shipped for English, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Korean, Mandarin, Portuguese, Spanish, Telugu. Of OTR's eight, en/es/fr/it/pt/zh/hi have shipped voices; ja is normalized but has no shipped default voice folder (my read of the file list).
- Speed: card reports, on one H100 (measured by Zyphra): 80 ms time to first audio at 8 concurrent requests, 457 s of audio per second peak throughput. No laptop or CPU real-time factor is published; the card says it runs real time on phones and laptops (claim, not measured by us).
- Sweet-spot build (int8 / int8_convrot): none. Only bf16 model.pth (1.77 GB) plus GGUF "ptq" files (zonos3-ptq.gguf 663 MB; also zac.gguf 2.2 GB, ssl-teacher 1.5 GB, zac-encoder 4.0 GB) for its own ggml runtime. No ComfyUI-core loader exists for this format.
- 16 GB fit: estimate, easily (under 5 GB of GGUF files in total). 8 GB fit: plausible; ggml CPU/Vulkan runtime exists.
- ComfyUI: no core support; a registry search for "zonos" shows only ZONOS2 packs (Active) and no ZONOS3 pack. Runs via Zyphra's own CUDA/MLX/ggml runtimes (https://github.com/Zyphra/ZONOS3-Mini) or SGLang-Omni.
- Licence: Apache-2.0, ungated.
- Why it matters: small, cloning, near-real-time, covers most of OTR's languages. Risk: not wired to ComfyUI; OTR would call a ggml CLI/HTTP server outside the core loaders. The probe is a standalone one: run the ggml CLI on the 5080 and the 4060 CPU, measure real-time factor and listen.

### VoxCPM2 (OpenBMB) -- WATCH (new to SEEN; model itself is older than today)
- Link: https://huggingface.co/openbmb/VoxCPM2 (card read; listed as updated 2026-11-09, downloads about 989k).
- 2B params, 30 languages including all eight OTR languages (en, es, fr, it, pt, ja, zh, hi), voice design from a text description, cloning, 48 kHz output. Published speed (by the authors): RTF about 0.3 on an RTX 4090, about 0.13 with Nano-VLLM.
- Sweet-spot build: none found (single bf16 model.safetensors 4.58 GB plus audiovae.pth 377 MB). Candidates for a repack: Comfy-Org (not seen).
- ComfyUI: community packs, not core: ComfyUI-VoxCPM2 and ComfyUI-FL-VoxCPM (both Active in the registry, April 2026). Licence Apache-2.0, ungated.
- 16 GB fit: estimate fits (about 5 GB of weights). Risk: heavier than Kokoro; RTF 0.3 is on a 4090, not measured on the 5080. Would be the cloning-plus-eight-languages test if ZONOS3 disappoints.

## Video
### HyperFlow H3 Flash INT8 repack (BennyDaBall) -- WATCH (update to the 2026-11-07 HyperFlow row)
- Link: https://huggingface.co/BennyDaBall/HyperFlow-H3-Flash-INT8-ComfyUI (2026-11-09; card read).
- What changed: first int8_convrot build of HyperFlow H3 Flash on ComfyUI's native int8 kernels: minimax_h3_fl2va_hyperflow_flash_int8_convrot 15.9 GB and minimax_h3_ref2va_..._int8_convrot 16.3 GB, ungated, workflows included. Card reports (measured by the author, one RTX 5090 32 GB): 46 s for a 5.2 s 1344x768 clip with audio in 6 steps; peak VRAM 23.6 GiB (nvidia-smi, whole GPU).
- 16 GB fit: no. 23.6 GiB peak is above the 5080's 14.5 GB ceiling; the file alone is 15.9 GB. Licence: MiniMax H3 community licence ("other"). Verdict WATCH: the int8_convrot format is the sweet spot, but the size is not 16 GB; needs a pruned build (like minimax_h3_ref2va_pruned_int8_convrot) to matter.
- Other H3 derivatives seen in trending (TenStrip/10Eros-Max, Open-H3-Regenerate-2K-IC and its ComfyUI repack, H3 temporal-window tiles pack): all H3-based, not read this run.
