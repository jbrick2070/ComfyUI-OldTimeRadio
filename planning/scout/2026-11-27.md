## Upscalers
**Kandinsky SR 2.0 (image + video), community int8_convrot repack and native nodes -- TEST (re-reported: material change).** Earlier SEEN line (11-26, WATCH) said no int8 and no ComfyUI path; both changed on 11-26/27.
- Source: https://huggingface.co/PedroMarinhoDev/Kandinsky-SR-ComfyUI (card read, MIT, ungated; weights byte-identical to kandinskylab's release except the int8_convrot quantization) and https://github.com/PedroMarinhoDev/ComfyUI-KandinskySR (README read; registry id kandinsky-sr-native 1.0.0, 2026-11-26). Base repos: https://huggingface.co/kandinskylab/Kandinsky-ImageSR-2.0 and the Kandinsky-6.0-VSR-2.0-distilled2steps model.
- What it does: ImageSR: one-step x2/x4 still upscaling with a creativity setting. Video SR: x2, x2.25, x4 video upscaling (a 2-step distilled model). It is a real candidate for the 8 GB LTX lanes whose picture stays 832x480 (apple/VIDEO_MODELS.md) and for stills; what ships today is the rtx_vsr engine in the upscale_stage and the LTX latent spatial upscaler x2 on 16 GB lanes (config/workflow_matrix.json). It would be an alternative to compare against rtx_vsr, not a replacement.
- Sweet-spot build: YES, all ungated, same repo, sizes measured from the listing: diffusion_models/kandinsky-sr/kandinsky_image_sr_2.0_int8_convrot.safetensors 5.14 GB (bf16 9.06 GB); kandinsky6_vsr_2.0_distilled2steps_int8_convrot.safetensors 1.96 GB (bf16 3.19 GB). Also needed: the video KVAE 3.48 GB bf16 and, for images, the FLUX.1 VAE 0.34 GB.
- 16 GB fit: measured by the pack author on an RTX 4090 with fast kernels: image int8 x4 at 1024x1024 peaks 7.2 GB and takes 9.2 s (reference 18.3 s); video int8 15 s 960x540 to 1920x1080 takes 3.9 min at 10.5 GB. Fits 16 GB (their measurement on different silicon; ours not made). 8 GB: the image path peaks at 7.0-7.2 GB on their card, so the 4060 is borderline (my estimate); the 1.96 GB video model is the more plausible 8 GB candidate but its peak was only given for the bigger case.
- ComfyUI: custom node pack (kandinsky-sr-native, GPL-3.0, no pip installs), NOT core UpscaleModelLoader / spandrel. Benchmarked on ComfyUI 0.44; no minimum stated. Fast kernels need NVIDIA RTX 30-series or newer with Triton (Blackwell sm_120 not mentioned: unverified). Two other packs for the same weights exist (comfyui-ksr-unofficial, comfyui-kandinsky-image-sr-2-0; the official kandinsky6-sr is video-only).
- Risks: a creative-diffusion upscaler can invent detail and change faces (identity drift on character stills), one more custom pack GPL-3.0, Triton on Windows/Blackwell unproven, a community quantization with no published quality comparison vs bf16 (only the author's clips). Probe on faces from the existing character stills before trusting it.

## Writer LLMs (experimental)
**TimeCapsuleLLM-ja v4-chat (trtd56) -- WATCH.** https://huggingface.co/trtd56/TimeCapsuleLLM-ja-v4-chat . Update of the v3 already in SEEN (11-08).
- Base: trained from scratch (nanoGPT-style, custom model.py; n_layer 36, n_embd 1536, vocab 16,384, context 2048), 1.05B parameters per the card. Corpus: Japanese materials published 1868-1945 only (cutoff 1945), then 274 in-corpus Q&A pairs for an anneal; Apache-2.0.
- Formats: one model.safetensors 4.19 GB (about fp32, my reading of size / parameter count); no GGUF seen. Fits 16 GB easily (estimate: about 4.2 GB weights); runs via the repo's own model.py (transformers-less), so it is a hand-run experiment.
- Card, in my translation: not a modern chat model; it fabricates sources when it does not know; fact questions are answered about 7% of the time, but value/opinion questions ("how was X argued then?") about 60-75%; gate results published (zero post-war knowledge leaks in the app path, fabrication 11.7%). No sample sentence read (the samples sit in side files I did not open).
- Experiment: a Japanese (ja is one of OTR's eight languages) pre-1945 voice for a period radio drama script, with Kokoro ja voices. Main risk: from-scratch 1B, 2048 context, short coherent length is unproven.
