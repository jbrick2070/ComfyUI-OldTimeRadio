## Video
### LTX-2.5 Stage-1 2-step + Stage-2 1-step distill LoRAs (lvladikov) -- WATCH
- Link: https://huggingface.co/lvladikov/LTX-2.5-Stage1-Distill-2step-LoRA (card and file list read; appeared 2026-11-16).
- What it does: DMD2 LoRA on LTX-2.5 distilled so stage 1 runs in 2 steps instead of the 8-step teacher; a second, "highly experimental" LoRA makes the refine pass 1 step instead of 3 (picture only, close-ups and slow motion; fast motion can smear). Challenges the sampling cost of ltx25_high_video / foley / mime / audio_in lanes. Trained only at 576x320 and 320x576 stage 1 (1152x640 after refine), 5 s clips (author statement).
- Sweet-spot build: none needed or offered in int8 -- LoRAs only: ltx25_stage1_distill_2step_rank_64_lora_comfyui.safetensors 1.23 GB, ltx25_stage2_distill_1step_rank_128_lora_comfyui.safetensors 1.89 GB (bf16). The author's recommended 1-step refiner (NVIDIA SoL-Refiner-LTX-2.3-One-Step) exists as szwagros int8_convrot but the transformer is 23.5 GB: does not fit 16 GB. The stage-2 LoRA workflows avoid it.
- 16 GB fit: adds about 3 GB of LoRA on the base the lane already loads; no VRAM number published (none measured). Speed gain claimed by step count only, no wall-clock figure read.
- ComfyUI: core LoRA loader; the repo ships local workflow JSONs. Their SoL workflows need ComfyUI-LTXVideo (AdaIN); the stage-2 LoRA workflows need no pack. Licence: LTX-2.x Community License. Ungated.
- Risk: quality versus the 8-step teacher is shown only as sample clips by the author, no metrics; sound quality at 2 steps unverified. Proposed test: same beat on ltx25_foley_16gb, 8-step vs LoRA 2-step, judged by eye and ear.
- Also seen, already in SEEN or not relevant: Pika-H3-Flex, PAN2, HyperFlow, 10Eros, Frieren LoRA; Runway Praxis-1 (open weights, robotics world-action model, not a video generator for stories): SKIP.
