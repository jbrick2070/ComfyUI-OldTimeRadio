## Video
### HyperFlow H3 Flash (VideoRebirthAI) -- WATCH (re-report: material change vs the 2026-11-02 HyperFlow LoRA entry)
- https://huggingface.co/VideoRebirthAI/hyperflow-h3-flash (card read in part, updated 2026-11-06).
- What: Video Rebirth's compressed 14B version of MiniMax H3 (13.7B fl2va, 13.9B ref2va, under half of the ~31B transformer), 4/6/8 steps without CFG, video plus stereo audio. Would sit beside the shipped H3 fl2va and ref2va lanes and challenges them on size and step count.
- Sweet-spot build: PARTIAL. int8_convrot exists in the model's own repo: fl2va_int8_convrot/ three shards of 5.33, 5.29, 5.26 GB (about 15.9 GB total); ref2va_int8_convrot/ also. It is the authors' diffusers-style layout with their own runner (hyperflow_h3_flash_int8.py), not a Comfy-Org core file. The ComfyUI path is GGUF instead: Q4_K_M 9.05 GB, Q8_0 14.9 GB (all fl2va_gguf/ sizes listed on the card, 7.2 to 14.9 GB). Ungated.
- 16 GB fit: authors say GGUF is measured on 24 GB and 32 GB RTX cards and "16 GB GPUs are not measured yet". Weights alone: Q4_K_M 9.1 GB fits (estimate); the companion text encoder is large (qwen3vl_32b nvfp4_awq 15.7 GB from Comfy-Org/MiniMax-H3), so offload would be needed (estimate). 8 GB not plausible without heavy offload.
- Speed (author-stated): 92 s for a 5.2 s clip, 6 steps, RTX 5090; 36 s on H200. No 5080 Laptop figure.
- ComfyUI: needs custom packs ComfyUI-GGUF (city96) and ComfyUI-HyperFlow-Flash (Video-Rebirth); not core-loadable as shipped. Licence: MiniMax H3 Community License (territory limits listed on the card).
- Risk: needs two packs plus a 15.7 GB text encoder; quality versus the shipped H3 files unmeasured. The earlier HyperFlow 8-step LoRA entry stays valid as the core-loadable variant.

### Kandinsky S2V 1.0 (kandinskylab; Lite 2B, Pro 21B, plus distilled) -- WATCH
- https://huggingface.co/kandinskylab/Kandinsky-S2V-1.0-Pro-distill-Diffusers (card read; repos dated 2026-11-03); paper https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19290 and code https://github.com/kandinskylab/kandinsky-s2v seen in search results only.
- What: speech-to-video avatar generation. A reference frame plus a speech track animate the whole figure; the audio is a condition, no sound is produced. 125 frames at 25 fps per 5 s chunk, longer speech chained chunk by chunk (FramePack memory). Relevant to the audio-conditioned video interest; it would challenge the H3 ref2va audio-in lane for speaking characters, not add a beat generator.
- Sweet-spot build: none yet. Pro-distill transformer 39.8 GiB, text encoder 16.6 GiB (Qwen2.5-Omni thinker); Lite-distill transformer about 4.2 GiB (from a search-result listing, not read on the card). Formats offered: bf16 diffusers only. Plausible makers: Comfy-Org or a community repack.
- 16 GB fit: Pro does not fit (author peak about 50 GiB with offload on an 80 GB card; GitHub README per search summary: 46 GiB distilled Pro, 57 GiB Lite, about 28 GB with VAE tiling). Lite-distill might fit 16 GB, unmeasured (estimate).
- ComfyUI: none found; the card says it is not in a released Diffusers version yet (modular pipeline). MIT licence, ungated.
- Risk: no ComfyUI path, large text encoder, unmeasured quality for stylised radio-drama characters; consent note on the card.
